Truth before signals

Every number needs a state.

Missing information should remain missing. A verified zero is not the same as an unknown value.

VERIFIEDEvidence checked and accepted for its stated purpose.
DELAYED / STALEData exists, but timing limits how it should be interpreted.
PENDING / REVIEWEvidence or owner decision is not complete.
UNAVAILABLE / UNKNOWNNo valid claim can be made from the current information.
